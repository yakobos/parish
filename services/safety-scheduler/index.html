ONE SUNDAY AT A TIME · SATURATING COUNTERS

Usher Team Scheduler

Every week is a real Sunday, solved against the full ledger of Sundays before it. Workload fairness uses a saturating counter rather than a lifetime total, so a new usher steps into the rotation level with everyone else instead of owing catch-up shifts — and joining also resyncs the pairing ledger so nobody is left permanently out of step.

Saturating counters

Both counters roll over on the same rule: when every entry is at least one, subtract one from all of them.

Workload counter

Each usher's share of the current rotation. It never accumulates debt, which is why a new usher is not favoured every week.

Pairing matrix

Times each pair has worked together since the last rollover. A zero beats a two, every time.

Staying in sync

Pair counts are relative, so adding an usher zeroes the whole ledger and the cycle restarts level. Nobody carries an unmatched row.

Master schedule

Weeks 1–5 are fixed baseline. Each later week is generated individually against every Sunday before it.
